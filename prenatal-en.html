The programme

Four modules, one complete journey

Each module is an immersion weekend, Saturday and Sunday from 9:00 to 18:00. Four weekends over four months to integrate what you learn between sessions.

Oct 2026 17 · 18

Module 1 · The changing body

Pregnancy anatomy and physiology. How the body changes trimester by trimester: hormonal, cardiovascular, respiratory and musculoskeletal changes. Pelvic floor, relaxin and everything a teacher needs to understand in order to support from knowledge, understanding and common sense.

Nov 2026 7 · 8

Module 2 · The practice that holds

How to prepare and teach a prenatal yoga class. Adapted Surya Namaskar, sequencing by trimester and chakras, working with specific conditions such as lower back pain or sciatica. Asana, pranayama, meditation, positive thinking and relaxation. The teacher's role and how to create the right atmosphere in class.

Dic 2026 12 · 13

Module 3 · Accompanying birth

How birth works and its mechanisms. Birth preparation through yoga: breathing, pain management and visualisation. How to create community and belonging, because a pregnant woman needs to feel held before, during and after. Contraindications and first aid if a student goes into labour during class.

Ene 2027 16 · 17

Module 4 · Postpartum and mums and babies

Postpartum yoga: contraindications, pelvic floor and abdominal recovery, hypopressives. How to structure a mums and babies yoga class. Bonding as practice. How to support women throughout the entire process, from the first trimester to the first months with their baby.

What you will learn

What you will be able to do

Pregnancy trimester by trimester

What happens in the body at each stage: physical, hormonal and emotional changes. What each change means for practice and how to adapt.

Anatomy and physiology

Cardiovascular, respiratory and musculoskeletal changes. Relaxin and its effects on joints and tendons. Abdominal diastasis.

Pelvic floor

Specific work during pregnancy and postpartum. Kegels, hypopressives and what to avoid at each stage. How to guide it with real knowledge.

Surya Namaskar, asana and pranayama

Sun salutation adapted to pregnancy, standing, seated, extension and inversion poses. Breathing techniques for practice and for birth.

Meditation and positive thinking

Relaxation, visualisation and mental tools to support women in experiencing pregnancy consciously and fully.

How to teach a prenatal class

Preparing the class, creating the atmosphere, adapting language, relieving specific discomforts. The teacher's role and how to support beyond the mat.

Contraindications

What to avoid during pregnancy and postpartum, when to refer and how to make decisions in class safely and with confidence.

Birth and its mechanisms

How birth works, what happens in the body, how to prepare women through yoga and what to do if a student goes into labour during class.

Postpartum yoga

Recuperación segura, suelo pélvico, faja abdominal. Contraindications y cómo volver a la práctica de forma progresiva.

Mums and babies yoga and building community

Classes with the baby present, bonding as practice, how to build community around motherhood. Supporting the whole journey from start to finish.

The trainer

Xana Dahdal

I was born in France and grew up in Portugal. I have been teaching yoga in Barcelona for over 14 years, with accreditation from the Generalitat de Catalunya and registered with ROPEC since 2015.

I have supported women through pregnancy and postpartum for years. This training comes from that accumulated experience and the desire to pass on what I know to those who want to do the same.

I teach through listening, adaptation and respect for the body. There is no single way to do things: there is the way that works for the person in front of you.
