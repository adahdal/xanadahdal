What is OMJOY

A proper party, no alcohol or drugs

OMJOY is a sober dance party and music gathering where joy, music, dancing and people take centre stage. The idea is simple: to show that you can have an authentic, joyful and memorable party without any substances.

You can dance however you like, chat with whoever is next to you, meet people, laugh, sit down for a rest and head back to the floor whenever you feel like it. There will be DJ sets across different styles, and nobody will ask you to be quiet or follow a choreography.

It is a gathering for adults, open to all kinds of people, whether you come alone, as a couple or with friends. It is not therapy and it does not promise any health effects: it is a party.

What OMJOY is not

  • It is not a yoga class
  • It is not a traditional ecstatic dance session
  • It is not a conventional party

The experience

What you will get

About the project

I am Xana. OMJOY comes from wanting to dance, talk and celebrate without alcohol or drugs.

I am Xana Dahdal, a yoga teacher in Barcelona. I am interested in music, movement, community and conscious celebration, and OMJOY is a way of bringing it all together in one place. I also DJ.

It is a new project and it is still taking shape. The name is provisional, and I will publish the details of each gathering here.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a sober party?

A party where nobody drinks alcohol or takes drugs. The energy comes from the music, the dancing and the people. OMJOY is an open, fun sober dance party: you dance, you chat and you celebrate.

Do I need to know how to dance?

No. There are no steps and no choreography. Everyone moves how they like, and you can also stay chatting or resting.

Can I talk during the event?

Yes. There are no rules about silence. Talking, laughing and meeting people is part of the experience.

What kind of music will I hear?

Music in different styles, with DJ sets. When each gathering is announced, I will publish which styles are playing and who is on the decks.

Is alcohol served?

No. OMJOY is a gathering with no alcohol and no drugs.

Can I come without knowing anyone?

Yes, you can come on your own. The idea is an open atmosphere, with room to chat and meet people at your own pace.

Can you organise private parties or events for companies?

Yes. I organise private sober parties, celebrations, community gatherings and corporate events, including team building with music and dancing. Tell me what you have in mind by WhatsApp or email.
