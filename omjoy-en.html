What is OMJOY

Back to the rhythm. Back to feeling

In many cultures and many eras, music and dance have been at the heart of celebration. A rhythm that repeats, a body that answers, and other people moving nearby, each in their own way. It is one of the simplest ways we have of being together.

OMJOY takes that idea into a party of today, with DJ sets across different styles. It is an invitation to listen to what is playing, notice how your body responds and be present for a while, unhurried.

It is a sober party: no alcohol and no drugs, with the energy coming from the music, the movement and the people who show up. It is a gathering for adults, open to everyone, whether you come alone, as a couple or with friends.

For everyone

You do not need to know how to dance

If you love music but think you cannot dance, this party is for you. Nobody here teaches steps or judges anyone: there are no levels, no choreography and no right way to move.

You can dance with your whole body or barely move, close your eyes, watch, laugh, chat, sit down and come back to the floor whenever you feel like it. You have nothing to prove to anyone. All you need is to like how it sounds.

About the project

I am Xana. OMJOY comes from wanting to dance, talk and celebrate without alcohol or drugs.

I am Xana Dahdal, a yoga teacher in Barcelona. I am interested in music, movement, community and conscious celebration, and OMJOY is a way of bringing it all together in one place. I also DJ.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to dance?

No. There are no steps, no levels and no choreography. Everyone moves how they like, and you can also stay chatting or resting.

What is a sober party?

A party where nobody drinks alcohol or takes drugs. The energy comes from the music, the dancing and the people. At OMJOY you dance, you chat and you celebrate.

Is it a class or a spiritual activity?

It is a party, with DJ sets and free dancing. It is not a yoga class or a traditional ecstatic dance session.

Do I have to take part in everything?

No. Everyone decides how and how much to take part.

Can I talk during the event?

Yes. There are no rules about silence. Talking, laughing and meeting people is part of the experience.

What kind of music will I hear?

Music in different styles, with DJ sets. When each gathering is announced, I will publish which styles are playing and who is on the decks.

Is alcohol served?

No. OMJOY is a gathering with no alcohol and no drugs.

Can I come without knowing anyone?

Yes, you can come on your own. The idea is an open atmosphere, with room to chat and meet people at your own pace.

Can you organise private parties or events for companies?

Yes. I organise private sober parties, celebrations, community gatherings and corporate events, including team building with music and dancing. Tell me what you have in mind by WhatsApp or email.
