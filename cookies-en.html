Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They let the site remember your preferences and measure how it is used. This website also saves some data in the browser storage (local and session), which we treat the same way as cookies.

2. What this website uses

The first time you visit, a notice lets you choose Accept all, Reject all or Settings by category. If you reject, only what is strictly necessary is used. Analytics and advertising tools load only if you accept them.

Type Name Purpose Duration
Necessary xd_consent_v2 (local storage) Save your cookie choice 6 months, then you are asked again
Necessary xd_last_lang (session storage) Remember the language when a form is sent Session
Analytics, only if you accept Google Analytics 4: _ga and _ga_Q0LPF6KP6H Count visits and page views, and measure clicks on WhatsApp and email and the information requests sent Up to 2 years
Analytics, only if you accept xd_lead_pending (session storage) Count each information request sent only once Session
Advertising, only if you accept Meta Pixel: _fbp, _fbc and fr Measure advertising on Facebook and Instagram Up to 90 days

Google Analytics is configured with advertising storage disabled and loads only with your analytics consent. Meta Pixel loads only with your advertising consent.

3. Third-party content

Some pages, such as the events page, include YouTube videos. This content is loaded by YouTube directly and may set its own cookies, even if you rejected analytics and advertising cookies. We recommend reading its privacy policy:

  • Google / YouTube privacy policy

4. How to manage cookies

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie preferences link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw your analytics or advertising consent, the corresponding cookies of this site are deleted and the page reloads.

You can also set your browser to block or delete cookies. Please note that this may affect how some parts of the site work.

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5. Consent

Necessary cookies do not require your consent. For analytics and advertising cookies we ask for your consent in the cookie notice, and you can accept one category and reject the other. For more on how we handle your data, see the privacy policy.

6. More information

For any question about the use of cookies, you can contact us at alexandradahdal@gmail.com.
