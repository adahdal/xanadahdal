A retreat as a necessary pause. Time away from the daily rhythm to deepen your practice, your silence and your relationship with yourself.

"A retreat is not a holiday. It is a real stop. And that, today, is harder and more necessary than ever."

I have organised retreats in old farmhouses, in natural settings, in places where the space already tells you something before the first class begins. Each retreat has its own energy.



What I create

Retreats I organise

When I organise a retreat I choose the space very carefully. The place matters. Practising in a farmhouse with centuries of history is not the same as anywhere else.

Each retreat has its own rhythm, its own intention and its own community. What matters is that there is real time for practice, for silence and for connection.

The programme combines asana practice, meditation, pranayama, yoga philosophy and free time. A careful balance between structure and freedom.

  • Size adapted to each proposal and space
  • Natural settings with history and character
  • Daily practice of asana, meditation and pranayama
  • Applied yoga philosophy
  • Conscious food included
  • Free time and integration

Upcoming retreats

Retreats are announced in advance on this website and on Instagram. Each edition has very limited places to preserve the quality and intimacy of the experience.

What I offer

Guest teacher
at retreats.

As well as organising my own retreats, I collaborate as a guest teacher at retreats organised by others. An opportunity to bring my practice and my way of teaching to different communities and contexts.

If you are organising a retreat and looking for a teacher, I can contribute in different ways: a class, a themed workshop, a talk on yoga philosophy, a meditation or pranayama session. Each collaboration is designed around the needs of the retreat and the group.

  • Over 14 years teaching in diverse contexts
  • Experience in national and international retreats
  • Teaching experience in Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Jivamukti and other styles
  • Prenatal yoga and mums & babies classes
  • Yoga philosophy and meditation workshops
  • Languages: Spanish, English, French and Portuguese
