Private

A practice designed for you, with personal attention and at your own pace. At home in Barcelona, in a studio or online.

A private class is yoga made to measure.
Your body, your level and your goals set the pace.

I am Xana Dahdal, a private yoga teacher in Barcelona with more than 14 years of experience. Whether you are starting from scratch or already practise and want to go deeper, I prepare each class around what you need right now.

Options

Three ways to practise with me

Yoga at home in Barcelona

I come to your home and we practise in your own space, at your pace and without you having to travel. A good option if you prefer to practise somewhere familiar.

Private classes in a studio

A one-to-one class in a studio, just you and me, with all the attention on your practice from start to finish.

One-to-one online classes

Live classes by video call, from your home or wherever you are. It works if you live outside Barcelona or prefer not to travel.

How I teach

A class that adapts to you

Everyone arrives with a different body, experience and goals. That is why no two classes are the same: I start from how you are today and build the practice from there.

My teaching includes Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga and other practices, and I combine them according to what you need.

  • If you are starting out, we learn the basics calmly and clearly
  • If you already practise, we can go deeper into postures, breathing or specific sequences
  • Style and intensity are adjusted to your level and your moment
  • Classes in Spanish, English, French or Portuguese
