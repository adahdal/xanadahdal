  • Pranayama, conscious breathing
  • Meditation

  • How to structure a class
  • Teaching methodology

  • Integrating everything learned

At the end

What you take away

I

Design your own classes

Structure and sequence classes with your own judgement, without copying templates or repeating what others do.

II

Hold groups with confidence

Guide and support a group with real presence. Knowing what to do when things do not go as expected.

III

Adapt practice to each person

Adapt yoga to different bodies, levels and life stages. Because no two bodies are the same.

IV

Teach from your own experience

Teaching that comes from what you have lived and understood, not from repeating what you were told.

The trainer

Xana Dahdal

I was born in France and grew up in Portugal. My connection with yoga started through a family member who practised Ashtanga. With over 14 years teaching in Barcelona, I have integrated styles such as Hatha Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Iyengar and Dharma Yoga.

I have taught in studios, companies, festivals and retreats. My teaching is based on listening, adapting and respecting the body. I do not teach what I was told to teach. I teach from what I have lived.

I hold the professional accreditation from the Generalitat de Catalunya and have been registered with ROPEC since 2015.
