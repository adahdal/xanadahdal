The approach

Wellbeing is also part of work

We spend many hours working and do not always notice how that routine affects us. Long periods sitting down, accumulated tension, a lack of breaks or communication difficulties can all influence how we feel day to day.

My aim is to bring companies practical tools that people can understand, experience and use in their daily lives. Activities can be organised as one-off sessions or as part of an ongoing programme.

Corporate yoga in Barcelona and online

Classes and movement

  • Corporate yoga

    Classes that combine postures, movement, breathing and relaxation. They can be adapted to people with no previous experience and to groups with different levels of practice.

  • Embodied Movement

    A dynamic practice of mobility, strength, stability, balance and coordination with a focus on body awareness.

  • Mobility and mindful conditioning

    Adapted exercises to develop functional strength, mobility and stability, respecting each participant’s abilities.

  • Active breaks

    Short movement and mobility sessions that can take place during the working day.

  • Meditation and breathing

    Guided practices of mindful attention, breathing and relaxation.

How it works

Sessions take place at the company’s premises, in a meeting room, a dining area, a terrace or any available space. No special equipment is needed.

They can be scheduled before the working day starts, during a break or at the end of the day, in person or live online.

Training for companies

Learning tools we can actually use

The courses combine theory, practical exercises and situations related to the workplace. The aim is for each participant to understand what they are learning and be able to apply it afterwards.

  1. Stress management and emotional self-regulation

    Recognising the usual signs of stress, understanding how they affect the body and attention, and practising tools to respond better to tense situations.

  2. Mindfulness and attention

    Training attention, noticing distractions and learning simple meditation exercises that can be built into the working day.

  3. Breathing and stress management

    Understanding the link between breathing and the stress response, and practising breathing techniques adapted to different situations.

  4. Mindful movement and postural habits

    Learning about movement habits, doing mobility exercises and developing greater body awareness during the working day.

  5. Positive and assertive communication

    Working on active listening, expressing needs clearly, feedback and difficult conversations.

  6. Conflict prevention and management

    Understanding how disagreements arise and learning communication strategies and constructive ways of handling tense situations.

Formats

About me

Xana Dahdal

Yoga teacher and trainer since 2011

I started practising yoga in 2005 and, over time, that practice also became my profession. Since 2011 I have taught people with different levels of experience, from beginners to advanced practitioners, and for years I have been part of yoga teacher training programmes.

How I teach has always mattered to me. I want people to understand what they are doing, to be able to observe their own processes and to find tools that are useful for their practice. That experience is the basis of my work with companies, where I adapt each session to the group’s needs and the context of the activity.
