Classes and movement

  • Corporate yoga

    Classes that combine postures, movement, breathing and relaxation. They can be adapted to people with no previous experience and to groups with different levels of practice.

  • Embodied Movement

    A dynamic practice of mobility, strength, stability, balance and coordination with a focus on body awareness.

  • Mindful movement, mobility and conditioning

    Adapted exercises to develop functional strength, mobility and stability, respecting each participant’s abilities.

  • Active breaks

    Short movement and mobility sessions that can take place during the working day.

  • Meditation, mindfulness and breathing

    Guided practices of mindful attention, breathing and relaxation.

How it works

Sessions take place at the company’s premises, in a meeting room, a dining area, a terrace or any available space. No special equipment is needed.

They can be scheduled before the working day starts, during a break or at the end of the day, in person or live online.

Sessions are adapted to the group’s level, from people with no experience at all to regular practitioners. The goal is for everyone to be able to take part.

Training for companies

Learning tools we can actually use

The courses combine theory, practical exercises and situations related to the workplace. The aim is for each participant to understand what they are learning and be able to apply it afterwards.

  1. Stress management and emotional self-regulation

    Recognising the usual signs of stress, understanding how they affect the body and attention, and practising tools to respond better to tense situations.

  2. Mindfulness in the workplace

    Training attention, noticing distractions and learning simple meditation exercises that can be built into the working day.

  3. Breathing and stress regulation

    Understanding the link between breathing and the stress response, and practising breathing techniques adapted to different situations.

  4. Mindful movement and postural wellbeing

    Learning about movement habits, doing mobility exercises and developing greater body awareness during the working day.

  5. Positive and assertive communication

    Working on active listening, expressing needs clearly, feedback and difficult conversations.

  6. Conflict management

    Understanding how disagreements arise and learning communication strategies and constructive ways of handling tense situations.

Formats
  • 2-hour workshops
  • 4-hour courses
  • Tailored programmes of 6 to 8 hours or several sessions
  • In person and live online

Why at work

What it can bring to your team

We spend many hours working and do not always notice how that routine affects us. Long periods sitting down, built-up tension, a lack of breaks or communication difficulties can all influence how we feel day to day.

  • I

    Less built-up tension

    Breathing and mindful movement offer tools to slow down and respond better to everyday pressure.

  • II

    Back and posture care

    Long hours sitting and work postures put strain on the neck, shoulders and lower back. Sessions work on mobility, strength and postural awareness.

  • III

    More focus

    Breathing and mindfulness techniques help train focus and presence.

  • IV

    A more cohesive team

    Doing something together that has nothing to do with work creates a different kind of bond.

  • V

    Better atmosphere

    When a company takes care of its people, people notice, and it shows in the day-to-day atmosphere.

  • VI

    A clear signal

    Offering yoga says something about how you treat people. It shows that wellbeing is more than a word in the company policy.

FAQ

What companies usually ask me

Do people need any previous experience?

No. Sessions are adapted to the group’s level, from people who have never practised to regular practitioners.

Do we need equipment or a special room?

No special equipment is needed. Sessions can take place in a meeting room, a dining area, a terrace or any available space.

Where do the activities take place?

In Barcelona and the surrounding area, at your premises or another venue, and also live online.

How long are the sessions?

Classes can last 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Training workshops last 2 hours, courses 4 hours and tailored programmes 6 to 8 hours or several sessions.

Which languages do you teach in?

Spanish, English, French and Portuguese.

Can the training be subsidised through FUNDAE?

In Spain, some training activities may be eligible for FUNDAE subsidies, provided they meet the requirements and the company has training credit available. The company manages the subsidy and the eligibility of each course must be checked beforehand.

How much does it cost?

Each quote is prepared individually, depending on the type of activity, length, frequency and number of participants.
