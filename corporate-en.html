Yoga as occupational
health prevention.

In Spain, musculoskeletal disorders account for over 30% of recognised occupational diseases. Sedentary work, forced postures and sustained mental load are real risk factors that yoga addresses directly.

A yoga programme in the workplace can be integrated into the Occupational Health and Safety Plan as a preventive measure against:

  • Musculoskeletal disorders from sedentary work
  • Neck and lower back tension from work postures
  • Burnout syndrome and chronic stress
  • Mental fatigue and lack of concentration
  • Anxiety and difficulty switching off
