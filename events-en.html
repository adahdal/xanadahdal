Collective energy

"Teaching at a festival is unlike anything else. People arrive with a different energy, open. And that changes everything."

When I teach at a festival I know I have people from different countries in front of me, with different levels, who may have never done yoga before. I teach Hatha, Hatha Vinyasa and Ashtanga, and I can adapt any class if there is a pregnant woman or a postnatal mother in the group. I communicate in Spanish, English, French and Portuguese, and I connect easily with international groups.

What I offer

What can I bring
to your event?

Large group yoga classes

Yoga sessions adapted to large groups, from 50 to 100 people or more. Accessible format for all levels, with energy and presence.

Themed sessions

Sunrise yoga, yoga with live music, meditation and movement sessions. Each proposal adapted to the spirit of your festival or event.

Yoga for families and all audiences

I can adapt my classes to any audience, including families, beginners or mixed-level groups. Whatever your festival needs, I make it work.

Workshops

Smaller, deeper workshops for participants who want to go further. Yoga philosophy, pranayama, meditation or chakra work.

Artistic collaboration

Yoga as performance, as an event opening, as a collective experience integrating music, movement and presence.

Flexible format

I adapt to your space, your duration and your audience. Indoors or outdoors, morning or afternoon, with or without live music. Tell me your idea.
