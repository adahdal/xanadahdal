This is not just about learning to teach yoga. It is a process of understanding the body, the practice, and how to hold space for others from real experience.

"Perhaps you have been practising for a while… but feel there is something deeper you have not yet fully integrated. Or perhaps you already teach… but do not always feel fully confident holding a class."

This training starts from there. A space to integrate, refine and develop a practice and a way of teaching that is truly yours.



Who it is for

This training is for you if…

01

You want to deepen your practice

You have been practising for a while and feel there is a deeper dimension you have not yet fully integrated.

02

You feel yoga is more than postures

You sense the practice goes far beyond the physical body and want to explore that dimension with guidance and structure.

03

You already teach and want more confidence

You teach classes but do not always feel fully confident holding a group. You want to teach with more clarity and presence.

04

You want to teach from within

Your goal is not to repeat sequences, but to teach from a genuine understanding of the body and your own experience.

Content

The programme

  • Chakra work

  • How to structure a class

  • Yoga Sutras

Integration and real practice

  • Observing and refining your teaching
  • Integrating everything learnt

Who we are

The teachers

Xana Dahdal

Yoga Teacher · +14 years

Born in France and raised in Portugal, Xana came to yoga through a close family member who practised Ashtanga. With over 14 years teaching in Barcelona, she has taught Hatha, Hatha Vinyasa and Ashtanga in studios, companies, festivals and retreats. Her teaching is grounded in listening, adapting and respecting the body.

Benji Frayce

Yoga Teacher · Reiki Master

His path in yoga began in Peru and continued in India, where he lived and studied at a traditional Hatha Yoga ashram. Certified teacher of Hatha Raja and Power Yoga, Reiki master and sound therapist. He currently runs his own studio in Barcelona and teaches in French, Spanish and English.
